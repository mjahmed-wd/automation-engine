# Automation Engine — Privacy Policy

## Summary

Automation Engine does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to any external server.

## How automation works

When you run a script, the extension uses the Chrome DevTools Protocol to interact with the active browser tab. The contents of the pages you automate are processed only inside your browser and are never sent anywhere. Script output appears in the side panel and is discarded when you close it.

## Saved scripts

Saved scripts are written to `chrome.storage.local`, which is stored on your device. They never leave your browser.

You can clear them at any time from **chrome://extensions** → Automation Engine → **Site Data**, or by uninstalling the extension.

## Network activity

The extension makes no network requests of its own. The only outbound traffic is whatever the pages you choose to automate would have made anyway.
